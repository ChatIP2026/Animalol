# Carousel Pattern Bank

Fill-in structures with one worked example each.

## Slide-1 Hook Patterns

- **Numbered stakes:** "[N] [things] that keep [audience] [pain state]" + a tease — *"5 pricing mistakes that keep freelancers broke. (I made all 5 — #4 cost me a year's rent.) →"*
- **Bold reframe:** "[Common belief] is wrong. Here's what's actually happening →" — *"'Post more' is wrong advice. Here's what growing accounts actually do →"*
- **The callout:** "[Audience]: stop [common action]" — *"New managers: stop answering every question →"*
- **Stat jolt:** "[Surprising number]. Here's why →" — *"80% of your revenue is 3 clients. Here's why that's dangerous →"*
- **Cut-off sentence:** a line that grammatically requires the swipe — *"The day I raised my rates, my best client said something I'll never forget —"*

## Mini-Loop Transition Lines (end of a slide)

- "...but the next one costs you even more →"
- "...and that mistake causes the next one →"
- "...which works — until [condition] →"
- "There's one rule that ties all [N] together →" (into the payoff slide)
- "The fix takes 10 seconds — next slide →"

## Value-Beat Micro-Structure (one slide)

Claim → why → fix/proof, ~2-second skim:
> **Mistake 3: Quoting before scoping.**
> "Roughly how much?" is a trap — your first number becomes the ceiling.
> Fix: "I'll send a quote once I understand the scope — 24 hours."

## Final-Slide CTA Patterns (by goal × platform)

- **Save (Instagram):** "Save this for the next time [trigger moment]. 🔖" — *"Save this for your next quote 🔖"*
- **Comment (LinkedIn):** "Which [item] have you caught yourself doing? Tell me in the comments." (Promise to reply — and reply.)
- **Share:** "Send this to the [person] still [mistake]."
- **Follow:** "I break down [topic] for [audience] every [cadence] — follow for the next one."

## First-Caption-Line Hooks (its own loop, feed-visible)

- The story the slides compress: *"I undercharged for 2 years before I saw the pattern."*
- The contradiction: *"The client didn't reject my price. He rejected how I said it."*
- The cost: *"Mistake #4 alone cost me a year's rent."*
