# Email Pattern Bank

Fill-in structures with one worked example each. Subject + preview work as a pair — the preview extends, never restates. All numbers illustrative; real work uses intake numbers only.

## Subject + Preview Pairs by Formula

### Curiosity gap
- S: "the mistake hiding in your [thing]" / P: names the cost or the fix's territory without closing the gap
  — *S: "the mistake hiding in your morning routine" / P: "It's not the snooze button — it's what you do at minute 11."*
- S: "what [group] never tell you about [topic]" / P: the stake
  — *S: "what landlords never tell you about deposits" / P: "One photo can get your full deposit back."*

### Emotional mirror
- S: "[the reader's private objection, verbatim]" / P: the reframe
  — *S: "you don't need talent to paint this" / P: "Water does half the work — I'll show you the other half."*
- S: "if [feeling] hits every [time]…" / P: the promise
  — *S: "if Sunday dread hits every week…" / P: "Three planning tweaks and Monday stops looming."*

### Specificity
- S: "how I [result] with [oddly specific detail]" / P: the surprising part
  — *S: "how I doubled replies with a 4-word subject" / P: "It broke every rule I teach."*

### Stakes
- S: "don't [common action] until you read this" / P: what's at risk
  — *S: "don't renew that software until you read this" / P: "Most teams pay for 3 seats they never use."*

### Pattern break
- S: "forget [common advice]" / P: the replacement
  — *S: "forget morning routines" / P: "The highest performers I know optimize their shutdown instead."*

### Question that's "about me"
- S: "is your [thing] secretly [problem]?" / P: the tell-tale sign
  — *S: "is your pricing secretly scaring clients off?" / P: "One line in your proposal gives it away."*

## Opening-Line Patterns (earn the read)

- **Quoted objection:** open with the reader's own words in quotes — *"'I'd love to write, but I'm not a writer.'"*
- **Scene drop:** mid-scene, no preamble — *"It's 11pm and you're still reformatting the deck."*
- **Confession:** a credibility-building admission — *"I almost didn't send this email."*
- **The number:** a bare stat that demands explanation — *"Forty-one days. That's how long the average gym habit lasts."*
- **Callback:** continue the subject line's sentence — S: "the mistake hiding in your portfolio" → first line: *"It's on your very first slide."*

## CTA Lines (one action, low friction)

- Reply: "Hit reply with one word — [word] — and I'll send it over."
- Click: "[Verb the outcome] →" (*"Fix your pricing →"* beats *"Click here"*)
- Book: "Grab one of the [N] spots" (only if N is real)
- Launch P.S.: "P.S. [Deadline/price fact, only if true]. [Same CTA link]."
